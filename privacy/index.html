Petsofy App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who we are

Petsofy ("we", "us") operates pet supply stores in the United Arab Emirates (petsofy.ae) and Kuwait (petsofy.net), and the Petsofy mobile app.

Email: petsofykwt@gmail.com Phone: +965 97151577

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following on your phone only. It is not sent to us and we cannot read it:

  • Which store you chose (UAE or Kuwait)
  • Your chosen colour theme
  • Your shopping cart
  • Products you saved to Favourites
  • Products you recently viewed

Clearing the app's data or reinstalling removes all of it.

What happens when you shop

Product listings, prices and stock come from our Shopify storefront. When you tap Checkout, the app opens Shopify's secure hosted checkout inside the app.

Any details you enter at checkout — name, email, phone, delivery address and payment details — are collected and processed by Shopify, not by the app. The app never sees or stores your payment information. Shopify's handling of that data is covered by their privacy policy: https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy

We receive the resulting order information (what you bought, delivery address, contact details) in order to fulfil and deliver your order.

Pet hotel bookings (Kuwait)

If you book a stay for your pet through the app, we collect the details you type in: your name and phone number, your pet's name, age and gender, your chosen dates, and any pickup or drop-off address. We use them only to run and confirm your booking and to contact you about it.

Signing in (Kuwait, optional)

Signing in is optional and only available for Kuwait. You enter your mobile number and we send a one-time code to your WhatsApp to confirm it is yours. We use your number to find the orders placed with it, and keep a sign-in token on your phone so you stay signed in; signing out removes it. We also keep a short record of code requests (your number and the time) to prevent abuse.

Your orders and checkouts

When you are signed in, the app shows the orders placed under your phone number and lists any checkout you started but did not complete (what was in your basket, its total and when you started it), so you can try again. We keep that record of started checkouts together with your account.

Saved details at checkout

When you are signed in and tap Checkout, we look up the name, email, phone and delivery address already held for you in our Shopify store and pass them to Shopify's checkout so you do not have to type them again. You can change them on the checkout page. We keep a short-lived copy of them to make this faster.

Product reviews

If you write a review, we store your rating, your text, your chosen display name and any photos you attach, and show them on the product page. The app asks for access to your camera or photo library only at the moment you choose to add a photo, and only for that photo. You can edit or delete your reviews in the app.

Notifications

If you choose to turn on notifications, the app registers your phone with us so we can send you offers and announcements. We store an anonymous notification identifier for your phone (supplied by Apple for iPhone or by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android) together with which store you chose. It is not linked to your name or any order, and we use it only to send you notifications.

Notifications are optional and off until you turn them on. You can turn them off at any time in the app under Settings, or in your phone's own settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not require you to sign in to browse or buy
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not access your contacts or microphone, and we use the camera and photos only when you choose to add a photo to a review
  • We do not sell your personal information
  • The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers

How long we keep order data

Order records are retained for as long as needed to fulfil the order and to meet accounting and tax obligations in the UAE and Kuwait.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about your orders by emailing petsofykwt@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the "last updated" date.
